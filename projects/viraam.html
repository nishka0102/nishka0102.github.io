Viraam

विराम

This project presents an outdoor furniture intervention designed for a specific site context, (Bund garden bridge in this case). The proposal considers the diversity of users and the range of activities occurring within the space, shaping the furniture’s form, scale, and functionality accordingly.

Target Users

User observations

  • Short-stay users (10–15 min) Commuters, passersby need quick rest, leaning support
  • Long-stay users (30–45 min) Families, friend groups need seating for conversation, shade, comfort

Users informally occupy railings, edges, and parapets for rest, socializing, and river-watching

The project is situated on Bund Garden Bridge in Pune, which is currently being redeveloped under the Pune Riverfront Development Project. The bridge is envisioned as part of a green urban corridor where pedestrians can pause, interact, and experience the riverfront.

Pipes for seating to maintain visual rythm

Early ideation focused on finding a form that could serve multiple postures without prescribing a single way of sitting, with modularity also being explored as a possibility.

Iterations moved from conventional bench typologies toward a more fluid, continuous surface , something that could accommodate both sitting and perching within a single unified form, while ensuring a non-invasive installation process and maintaining visual harmony with the existing bridge environment.
