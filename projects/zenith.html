The space offers an escape from urban routine, allowing residents to work remotely while enjoying natural tranquility and a strong sense of community.

The space is designed to evoke a calm yet productive atmosphere, allowing users to work in a peaceful setting away from the city.

The material palette is rooted in Sikkim’s local context, with bamboo textures, cane furniture and lepcha rugs chosen for their natural availability and traditional character.

While locally sourced slate anchors the space to its natural surroundings, and wooden flooring adds warmth and thermal comfort.
