This project presents a workspace-oriented storage solution for fine arts students, addressing the organization of personal items and workstation accessories. The design prioritizes adaptability and personalization, with materials and manufacturing strategies carefully considered for batch production.

Reconfigurable modular system adapting to different spatial arrangements

Concept development

The final concept evolved through iterative explorations, integrating modular storage, mobility, lockable compartments, height adjustability, brush-drying provisions, and hanging hooks. A height-adjustable glass palette allows color mixing, adapting to varied workstation heights and working postures.
