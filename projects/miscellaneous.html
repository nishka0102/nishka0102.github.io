The Great

Hornbill

This form study began with an exploration of the hornbill's tangible and intangible attributes where robust and regal stood out as the most defining qualities among a broader list.


Different poses of the bird were then studied and abstracted into sketches, and those best embodying the chosen attributes were developed into a 3-dimensional form capturing both strength and grace.

Regal & Robust

These exploratory projects focused on understanding design through form, material, and making.

From abstracting natural forms into 3D explorations to experimenting with thread-based techniques such as weaving and macramé, which encouraged the curiosity of how crafts can contribute to design, not only aesthetically, but also as functional elements.

Weaving

This exploration studies the Rush Weave technique, using two contrasting materials (Cotton cord & Jute thread) to create variation in texture and visual character. The interplay between the materials adds depth and creates a distinct overall look.
